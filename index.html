Posture monitoring, no hardware required

Your webcam already sees how you sit.

ThinkPulse watches your back and neck through your laptop camera: the forward head lean, the downward bend from looking at a phone or a low screen, the rounded shoulders. It ignores your legs entirely. Everything runs in your browser, and no video ever leaves your device.

Try the live demo

How it works

Three steps, all inside your browser tab.

01

Camera reads your position

A pose model finds your shoulders, ears, and nose in each video frame, on your device.

02

Angle gets calculated

The same two measurements physiotherapists use for forward head posture and rounded shoulders, adapted for a front-facing camera, are compared against your own upright baseline. Legs are not part of the score.

03

You get told, not scolded

A quiet status line changes when your posture slips, and a sitting timer tracks how long you've been at it.

Privacy

ThinkPulse's camera demo runs entirely client-side. Your camera stream is read directly by your browser and analyzed on your own device using a pose-detection model. This page explains exactly what that involves.

What we collect

  • Nothing is sent to a ThinkPulse server. This page has no backend and no database.
  • Your camera video is read by your browser, turned into posture numbers on your device, and then discarded frame by frame. No image or video is saved, screenshotted, or uploaded.
  • The sitting-limit minutes you set, and your upright-posture calibration, live only in this browser tab's memory. Close the tab and they're gone.
  • One small preference, whether you accepted or dismissed the cookie notice, is stored in your browser so we don't ask again. That's the only thing that persists.

Third-party embeds

This page loads a small number of resources from outside servers so it can render text and run the pose model:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for the typefaces.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) for the TensorFlow.js library and pose-detection model code.
  • The pose model itself downloads its trained weights from Google's model hosting the first time you start the camera.

None of these are asked to identify you and none of them receive your camera feed. Like any web request, though, they can see your IP address and browser details as a normal part of serving the file, governed by their own privacy policies rather than ours.

Children's data

ThinkPulse is built with students in mind, but this page does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and has no account system that would ask a child's age or identity.

Your rights

Because nothing is stored on our side, there is no ThinkPulse-held record of you to access, correct, or delete. If that changes as the product grows, this page will say so before it happens, not after.

Cookies

This page sets exactly one cookie: a record of whether you accepted or dismissed the notice at the bottom of the screen, so it doesn't reappear on your next visit. It carries no identifier beyond that choice.

What we don't use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No analytics or behavior-tracking cookies.
  • No cookies from the font or pose-model providers listed under Privacy; loading their files doesn't set a cookie on this page.
